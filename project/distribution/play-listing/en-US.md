## Full description
MyWeather brings worldwide weather forecasts, city search and interactive weather maps into an adaptive, easy-to-read experience, with Arabic and English interfaces.

Explore temperature, wind, humidity, precipitation and forecast details. Switch between interactive ECMWF cloud, rain and snow forecast layers, with model time and source information displayed in the app.

Find cities around the world, revisit saved places and choose your preferred units. Weather data comes from the providers identified within the app.

Coastal marine and tide estimates appear only where usable data is available. These are not surveyed harbour predictions and are not suitable for navigation. PyFES tidal predictions are not enabled in this release.

Weather forecasts are estimates, subject to change. For emergencies and safety-critical decisions, always use your local official weather authority.
